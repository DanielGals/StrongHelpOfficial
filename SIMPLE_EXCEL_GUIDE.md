# SUPER SIMPLE EXCEL GUIDE (No Skills Needed!)

## EASIEST WAY - Use Google Sheets (NO EXCEL NEEDED!)

### Step 1: Upload to Google Sheets (2 minutes)
1. Go to: https://sheets.google.com
2. Click: **Blank** (new spreadsheet)
3. Click: **File** → **Import** → **Upload**
4. Drag these 3 files:
   - TEST_CASES.csv
   - BUG_REPORT.csv
   - TEST_SUMMARY.csv
5. Done! ✅

### Step 2: Make It Look Good (1 minute)
**Just do these 3 things:**

1. **Make header bold:**
   - Click row 1
   - Click **B** button (makes it bold)

2. **Add colors:**
   - Select "Status" column
   - Click paint bucket icon
   - Pick green for "Pass"

3. **Share link:**
   - Click **Share** button (top right)
   - Copy link
   - Send to interviewer

**DONE! That's it!** 🎉
